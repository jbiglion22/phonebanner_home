Phone Banner Privacy Policy

English 한국어

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in Phone Banner (the "App"), a scrolling LED sign app for cheering at concerts, sports games and events. The App is developed by J.Biglion.

Summary
  • We do not ask you to create an account and do not collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • Your messages, presets and settings are stored in the App on your device. We do not run servers that receive them. Android backup or device transfer may include this data, depending on your device settings.
  • In Group Cheer mode, phones connect directly to each other over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Data exchanged for the cheer stays between the connected nearby devices and is not sent to us.
  • The sound reaction feature uses the microphone only to measure loudness while you have it turned on. Sound is not recorded, saved or sent.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob on some screens. No ads are shown on the full-screen playback screen.

The following data is created by you and saved in the App's storage on your device. It is not sent to us and we cannot access it. It stays on your device until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.

3. Group Cheer (nearby device connection)

Group Cheer uses Google Nearby Connections, part of Google Play services, to connect phones directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. An internet connection is not required.

  • Data exchanged between connected phones: room name, device model names, the cheer message and display settings, start time and stop signal, each phone's order and screen ratio, a random ID used only to recognize a phone that reconnects, and timing information used to synchronize clocks.
  • Who can see it: the room code and your device's model name may be visible to nearby devices using the App to search for rooms. The room name and cheer message are shared only with phones that join the room with the room code. The host can remove a phone from the room.
  • This data is exchanged only between the connected devices and is not sent to our servers. It is not kept after the session ends, except for messages you save as presets on your own device.
  • QR code join: the QR code contains only the room code.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob on some screens, such as the home, editing, template and My Banners screens and the exit dialog. No ads are shown on the playback, light stick or Group Cheer screens. A full-screen ad may appear before the light stick screen opens. Google and its partners may collect and use the following information to serve and measure ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the App.

The legal basis for ad-related processing is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. Data retention and sharing

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Uninstalling the App deletes its local data. Android backups may remain separately and may restore data when the App is reinstalled. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies. We do not sell or share personal data with any third party other than Google as described above.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
