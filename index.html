Features

Type, style, show

Two taps to play, no setup needed. Styling it your way is just as simple.

Instant play

Type on the home screen and tap ▶. That’s it — a full-screen landscape LED banner.

  • Tap a recent message to play it again
  • Long-press to edit or remove it

Make it yours

Five tabs — Text, Color, Motion, Effects and LED — to get exactly the look you want.

  • 7 text colors, background color, rainbow
  • Blink, glow, cycling background
  • Round, square, heart and star dots

Templates & team colors

Pick a ready-made message for baseball, soccer, concerts, birthdays, love notes or pickups — use it as is or tweak it.

  • 21 cheering templates
  • Recolor everything with one team color combo

Crisp, readable LEDs

Set the text size by vertical dot count — from big and chunky to fine and sharp, so every letter stays legible.

  • 16 to 48 dots tall
  • Dot size and unlit-dot brightness

My Banners

Save the banners you love and bring them back with one tap.

  • Favorites and recently used
  • Edit, duplicate, delete — with undo

Playback built for cheering

Nothing but the banner while it plays — plus a screen lock so it never closes by accident.

  • Max brightness, screen stays on
  • Speed control, pause, mirror mode
